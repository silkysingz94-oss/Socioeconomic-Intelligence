Gambia Night-Time Light & Socioeconomic Intelligence

Explore satellite-observed illumination alongside administrative geography and national socioeconomic indicators. Night-time brightness can provide useful evidence about settlement patterns, infrastructure and human activity, but it is not a direct measurement of GDP, household wealth or welfare. Interpretation requires independent demographic, economic and geographic information.

07 / SOCIOECONOMIC INTERPRETATION

Greater Banjul Area
Examine the relationship between illumination, urban density, commercial activity, transport networks and electricity infrastructure.
Urban and peri-urban settlements
Examine the transition between established urban centres and developing settlement corridors.
Rural Gambia
Lower illumination may reflect dispersed settlements, agricultural land or different electricity and lighting patterns.
Population and brightness
Compare population density with calibrated radiance. A high population area with low radiance deserves investigation, not an automatic classification as deprived.
Critical remote sensing

A dark area is not necessarily poor. A bright area is not necessarily wealthy. Industrial facilities, gas flaring, fishing activity, cultural practices and electricity access can influence satellite-observed illumination.
