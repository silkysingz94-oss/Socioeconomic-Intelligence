06 / SOCIOECONOMIC INTERPRETATION

Greater Banjul Area
Examine illumination patterns alongside settlement density, commercial activity, transport infrastructure and electricity access.
Urban and peri-urban settlements
Compare the spatial distribution of persistent illumination around established urban areas and developing settlement corridors.
Rural Gambia
Lower observed illumination may reflect dispersed settlements, agricultural land, lighting behaviour or electricity availability.
Population and brightness
Compare population density with independently calculated radiance. Low radiance should prompt further investigation, not an automatic classification of deprivation.
Remote-sensing caution
A dark area is not necessarily poor, and a bright area is not necessarily wealthy. Industrial facilities, fishing activity, gas flaring, cloud, atmospheric conditions and lighting practices can influence satellite observations.
