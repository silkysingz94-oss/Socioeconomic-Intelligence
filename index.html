Duplicate source rows are aggregated by administrative identifiers. Missing numeric observations remain missing and are not converted to zero.

METHODOLOGY

1. Load GBoS enumeration area boundaries.
2. Select an administrative geography.
3. Display NASA GIBS satellite imagery for the selected date, if available.
4. Upload zonal radiance statistics in CSV format.
5. Normalise geographic identifiers and aggregate repeated records.
6. Calculate descriptive summaries from valid uploaded values.
7. Export the resulting statistics and geographic features.
