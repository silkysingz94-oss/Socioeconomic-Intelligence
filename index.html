Reading The Gambia Through Night-Time Lights

Night-time satellite observations provide an additional perspective on human settlement, electrification, infrastructure and spatial development. This dashboard explores the distribution of artificial illumination across The Gambia using VIIRS satellite imagery. However, brightness is not a direct measurement of GDP, household wealth or quality of life. Interpretation requires demographic, cultural, economic and geographic context.

06 / CRITICAL REMOTE SENSING: THE GAMBIAN CONTEXT

Greater Banjul Area
Investigate whether concentrated illumination reflects urban density, commercial activity, transport networks and electricity infrastructure.
Urban and peri-urban settlements
Examine the transition between highly illuminated urban centres and developing settlement corridors.
Rural Gambia
Lower illumination may reflect dispersed settlements, agricultural land, lower infrastructure density or different electricity and lighting patterns. It should not be interpreted automatically as lower household welfare.
Socioeconomic validation
Compare radiance with census population, household characteristics, electricity access, economic activity and independently measured development indicators.
