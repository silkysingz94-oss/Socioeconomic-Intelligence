07 / RADIANCE STATISTICS
The displayed VIIRS GIBS imagery is a visual satellite layer. It is not a downloadable radiance raster for zonal statistics.

Mean radiance, maximum radiance and lit-pixel statistics are not calculated from the rendered map tiles. Upload a quantitative VIIRS raster or a verified zonal-statistics CSV to calculate those indicators.

09 / METHODOLOGY
1. Administrative boundaries are loaded from GeoJSON.
2. The selected administrative polygon is used as the clipping mask.
3. NASA VIIRS imagery tiles are rendered into browser canvas tiles.
4. Pixels outside the selected polygon are made transparent.
5. The mask is refreshed when the selected area or imagery date changes.

The displayed image is satellite visualization, not a substitute for calibrated radiance values.
